Micronutrients (from label)

Lab panel

Transcribe a dated panel from your lab report; leave anything not measured blank. Every value can also carry your lab’s own printed reference interval — enter it beside the value. This is a record of panels you already have; the app does not suggest which tests to get.
Where the targets come from, and how values are stored
Values with a cited Canadian target show it. Each value is stored as its own dated reading, so it trends like any other biometric.
Log a biometric or event
Weight, HRV, glucose, breath ketones, sleep, mood, energy, steps, BP; sauna, cold plunge, red light, HBOT, alcohol, … — on your daily timeline, never mixed into food totals.
Log a medication or supplement
Name is enough for a quick log; dose and the rest are optional. Kept structured (not buried in notes) so it can be correlated later.

Settings
Regimen
A regimen is a named daily template — medications, events, and preset meals at their times, with optional weekday rotation and an eating window. It pre-fills today's checklist at the top of the day; nothing is ever auto-logged. Author it as JSON (template below); food entries reference a saved preset's id.

        Fasting
        
The day ring draws eating in green and fasting in orange. From your last food — or from the time you tapped “Stopped eating” after it — the ring turns orange. Food logged within 12 hours turns that gap green, part of the eating window; 12 hours or more with no food is a fast, up to 48 hours; 48 hours or more with nothing logged is a gap in the log, not a fast. Nothing is asked.
